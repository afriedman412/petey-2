## What was built

- **'Belongs to another field', honed** (`petey/validate/witness.py`, `Names.rivals` / `judge`): a field's text
  is compared only with rivals (same page, within an inch, same XFA subform, no heavier rule between), needs a
  0.10 margin, measures fit both ways, and skips texts several fields carry word for word. Words are Snowball
  stems (`snowballstemmer`, added to requirements); the old five-letter cut joined employer / employee.

- **Text-match model** (`petey/label/text_match.py`): MiniLM-L6 cross-encoder fine-tuned on (text, field) pairs
  from the library, run with onnxruntime. Data `eval/text_match_data.py` (every reviewed form held out),
  training `eval/text_match_train.py` (separate venv `~/Documents/code/.venvs/petey-train`, PyTorch on MPS, one
  epoch ~13 min; ONNX inputs must go in forward() order), evaluation `eval/text_match_eval.py` (with Jev).
  Weights in `models/text_match/` (gitignored; a v1 backup in the worktree's `models/text_match_v1/`).

- **Combined witness**: the rules' or the model's 'belongs to', then the model's 'does not describe' (P < 0.01);
  a publisher flag contests the field only when the tooltip shares two thirds of its words with a line printed
  at the box (`printed_agrees`, Andrew's idea), otherwise it is 'unconfirmed' (a hint, not sent to the loop's
  reviewer). Readable widget paths are a publisher text only where there is no tooltip. Model on by default
  when its file exists; `PETEY_TEXT_MATCH=0` off.

- **Caption chooser** (`petey/label/caption_choice.py`): per option, the text printed around the box plus the
  tooltip part the siblings do not share, scored by the model against the option's meaning; unique per group;
  XFA and hand captions never replaced. `witness.caption_suggestion` shows proposals on the review page with a
  'use' fix. `group_review` copies a tooltip in as a caption only when it is 8 words or fewer.

- **Fixture** `eval/belongs_to_fixture.py`: the 21 hand-flagged name errors (`NAME_FLAGS`) and every reviewed
  field. Also `eval/caption_choice_eval.py`.

- Also: `witness.witnesses` no longer crashes on a `desc_type` rule without a fix; f720 grouping applied and
  committed; six judge-eval forms' saved grouping applied (`--no-page-captions`); README rewritten.

## Results (details in RESULTS)

- Name checks on reviewed fields: 1,667 flagged by the old tooltip check -> 157; real name errors caught 11 -> 15
  of 21; looked-at false alarms 22, about half of them real errors with an accept verdict.

- Model vs Jev on held-out forms: names 86.6% vs 87.0%, captions 89.1% vs 80.5%; ~95% right when both agree.

- Caption chooser: flagged groups 12/13, Andrew's corrections 31/34, 214 of 499 sentence captions get a short
  proposal, 92% of picks right on held-out options.
