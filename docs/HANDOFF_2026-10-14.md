## Tools built in this round

- **Working copy per form** (`petey/validate/draft.py`). Review, Groups and Add / remove all edit one copy; one "save" writes the blueprint and "discard" restores it. Review records follow page and Claude-pass changes.
- **Claude pass.**
  - `eval/claude_pass_dump.py` dumps a form; uncovered boxes appear as UNCLAIMED lines.
  - `eval/claude_pass_brief.md` is the reviewer brief.
  - `eval/claude_pass_apply.py` applies the sure changes into the working copy, records the unsure ones as suggestions with "use", moves verdicts with renames, and lets `asked_by: andrew` override locks.
- **`eval/yes_no_groups.py`.** Makes loose Yes / No checkbox pairs into one group each.
- **Name check.**
  - The "tells" rule: "belongs to another field" only when the text can tell the two names apart.
  - The bare item-number rule: `name_type.bare_number` flags names like `item_43f` and `line_112_amt`.
- **Review page.**
  - "Improve name" (one agent call).
  - Queue filters: unreviewed only, flagged only, Claude's changes only.
  - p and ← go back through the fields visited.
  - Group outlines on the page; clicking a box lights its key / caption row.
  - A louder selection outline.
- **Groups.** "Re-read captions" (agent with the page image) and "merge into another group" across pages.
- **Speed.**
  - Name-score cache lookups by key: 53 s → 1.4 s to open FAA 8710.
  - Witness inputs kept per blank: a re-check on CMS-855A dropped from 3.5 s to 0.8 s.

## Later on 2026-10-14: strategy, a validated batch, naming

This section supersedes "Next steps, in order" above.

### Strategy

Launch is 200+ validated forms, and generation is not part of the launch. The work is the cheapest way to validate, where the cost is Andrew's review time. Numbers are per page or per field, never per form: forms vary too much in size.

The modeling steps above (text-match retrain, local models, synthetic errors) serve the generation loop and wait.

### What the screens catch

Measured on the 19 validated forms as first generated, against 240 known name errors (`eval/triage_score.py`):

| screen | errors caught | fields to look at (of 5,370) |
|---|---|---|
| name checks | 69 | 145 |
| Jev below 0.5 | 130 | 821 |
| page check, name flags | 209 | 234 |

- The page check also holds up on the ten forms whose review never saw it: 96 of 109.
- It costs about 7 cents a page with Opus 5.
- The review rule that follows: look at what the page check (plus the name checks) flags, and regenerate forms where a large share of fields is flagged.

### Dropdowns

- **Format:** a dropdown carries `options: {key: {caption, stored}}`, like a group's options. `stored` is set only where the PDF stores something other than the key: CBP 7501 stores an index, CRA NR4 box 11 a code shifted by a blank first entry.
- **Generation:** the list's empty entry (a blank label, "Select one") is dropped in both choice readers.
- **Extraction:** the extractor maps a stored value back to its key.
- **Review page:** shows and corrects the entries like group options.
- **Applied:** only to the batch so far; 409 dropdowns elsewhere still carry a blank entry.

### Naming (the priority) and associated fields

- **The standard:** settled in `docs/NAMING.md` as `[description]_[iteration]_[suffix]_[part]`.
  - A number before the suffix is an iteration; anything after it is a part of one compound value.
  - The suffix list, and the description in the form's own words, are in the doc.
  - It goes into the BPT spec (section 5.2) once workshopped.
- **Standardizer:** `eval/standardize_names.py` writes copies to `runs/standardized/`. On the 19 validated forms plus DS-11, every non-group field is in shape, apart from 6 leftovers and 5 real duplicate clashes it lists.
- **Associated fields** (an ENTITY: fields about one thing; a COMPOUND VALUE: one value in several boxes, from a closed list of kinds) are a nice-to-have.
  - `eval/associate.py` (rules) puts 75% of fields in one.
  - `eval/associate_model.py` (one Opus call per form, about 3.5 cents a page) puts 84% in one.
  - Copies are in `runs/associations/`.
- **Applied so far:** nothing, to any real blueprint.
